One live palette. Routine feedback now uses the more playful NES-style jingles. Bigger studio/cartoon sounds are reserved for character choice, starting, the winning reveal, winning, and quitting.

Routine sounds are intentionally quieter and shorter than the source files. Major sounds are also reduced in volume so they act like punctuation rather than interrupting the game.
